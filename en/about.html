From creative direction to project management

I'm Marisol Garaycoechea, a graphic and sound designer (FADU) and web developer based in Buenos Aires.

I've spent 10+ years working across creative direction, photography, video production, motion graphics, and web development. These days I focus on project management, with an end-to-end view, and rounded that out with a Product Management program at UTN.

That range means when I lead a team, I understand firsthand what each stage involves, and where things tend to get stuck.
