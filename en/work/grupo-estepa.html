Grupo Estepa

Web Development · One Page — 2026

Institutional one-page site for a Patagonian strategic consultancy, built to convey credibility and regional identity. Grupo Estepa is a full-service consultancy for public and private organizations in Neuquén and Río Negro, Patagonia.

www.grupoestepa.com.ar → (opens in a new tab)

Context

Juana and Gero were launching their consultancy and needed a simple, fast site for the brand's debut, without losing the warmth and regional Patagonian identity that sets them apart.

My role

Research, content writing, and full site development. The logo and visual identity are by Camilo Vázquez Wlasiuk.

What I did

  • Custom content: I started by talking through the project with them and wrote the copy around their goals, mission, and vision. This wasn't filling in a template, it was writing the voice of a consultancy just getting off the ground.
  • One page, well organized: work areas, methodology, team, and contact laid out in a single continuous scroll, alternating clear cream sections with dark blocks in the brand's signature estepa green.
  • Plain HTML, CSS, and JS, no CMS: for a launch site that wasn't going to need constant content updates, going static was the right call. It loads faster, needs no upkeep, and ships faster too.
  • Contact built in: the contact form and direct WhatsApp and email channels sit at the end of the scroll, as the natural close to the site's story.
