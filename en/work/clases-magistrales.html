Clases Magistrales

Video · Editing and Motion Graphics — 2022

Editing and motion graphics for an episode of the documentary series produced by Agencia I+D+i (Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation), available on its Pulsar virtual campus. I worked on this project at El Maizal.

Context

Clases Magistrales presents, episode by episode, lectures given by doctoral professors from universities across the country. The series was produced by Agencia I+D+i, part of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, in 2023, and distributed on its virtual campus, Pulsar.

My role

Editing and motion graphics for a full episode of the series, plus the series intro.

Visual language

Motion graphics as the main resource to support the academic content and carry the episode's pacing.
