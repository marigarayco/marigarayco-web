Video · Photography — 2021–2024

Photo campaigns and video pieces for Rayito de Sol, alongside Agustina Roasio.

Context

For each campaign, we start by understanding the goal, look into trends, and lock a moodboard and location before shooting.

My role

Production, photography, camera, and editing. The images went on to be used on the brand's website, and we also produced their product tutorial videos.
